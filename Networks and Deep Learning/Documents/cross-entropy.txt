The statement should be understood as:

Cross-entropy removes the sigmoid derivative from the output-layer error δL, not from the hidden-layer errors δl.

This matters particularly because the output layer is where the network directly 
compares its prediction with the target. With quadratic cost, a badly wrong but 
saturated sigmoid output can produce a very small gradient. Cross-entropy prevents that particular source of slowdown.

It does not magically solve vanishing gradients throughout a deep sigmoid network. The hidden-layer factors 
σ′(zl) remain, which is one reason modern networks generally use activations such as ReLU rather than sigmoid in hidden layers.
